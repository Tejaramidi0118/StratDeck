    📜 StratDeck Game Rules & Moves Guide

    🏆 Objective & Core Win Condition

    StratDeck is a high-stakes tactical survival card game (Russian Roulette / Exploding Kittens mechanic). Your ultimate goal is to be the last player alive.

    Lurking hidden inside the draw deck is a lethal HITMAN bomb card. Every time a player draws from the deck, they risk drawing the HITMAN. If you draw the HITMAN and do not hold a SHIELD in your hand, you are instantly eliminated and lose the match!

    ⏱️ Core Turn Rule: One Action Per Turn

    StratDeck enforces a strict, fast-paced one action per turn rule. Whatever you choose, your turn ends immediately after playing:

    ▶ OPTION A: PLAY 1 CARD

    Play any 1 action card from your hand (such as ATTACK, SKIP, SPY, STEAL, SWAP, or TRAP).

    Its effect triggers immediately, and your turn ends on the spot without drawing.

    🎴 OPTION B: DRAW 1 CARD

    Click DRAW CARD to draw the top card from the deck into your hand.

    Your turn immediately concludes. (Watch out: drawing carries the lethal HITMAN hazard risk!)

    🃏 Complete Card Roster & Move Breakdown

    Here is what every card and move does in the game:

    💣
    HITMAN Lethal Threat (In Deck)
    The elimination bomb hiding in the draw deck. If drawn, you are eliminated on the spot unless you have a SHIELD in your hand to auto-defuse it. Cannot be held in your starting hand.
    🛡️
    SHIELD Passive Life Saver / Skip
    Passive Effect: Keep in hand! If you draw the HITMAN, SHIELD automatically defuses the explosion and saves your life. Active Play: Can be played from hand to skip your draw (ends turn), but consumes your defense.
    ⚔️
    ATTACK Offensive • Ends Turn
    Immediately ends your turn without drawing and forces your opponent to take 2 consecutive turns! They must take multiple life-or-death draws from the deck or burn avoidance cards to survive.
    🛑
    SKIP Avoidance • Ends Turn
    Immediately ends your turn without drawing any card from the deck. Crucial when the deck size is small and Hitman risk is dangerously high.
    🔁
    REVERSE Direction Switch • Ends Turn
    Reverses turn order in multiplayer matches. In a 1v1 duel, functions identically to a SKIP, safely concluding your turn without drawing a card.
    🪤
    TRAP Hazard • Ends Turn
    Arms a hidden trap on the draw deck and ends your turn without drawing. When your opponent takes their next draw, they are forced to draw an extra card (2x draw penalty!).
    👁️
    SPY Tactical • Information
    Peeks at the top 3 cards of the draw deck in exact sequential order, then ends your turn without drawing. Gives you certainty on whether the HITMAN is lurking near the top.
    💰
    STEAL Tactical • Resource Theft
    Steals 1 random card directly from your opponent's hand into yours, then ends your turn without drawing. Deprives the opponent of their shields while expanding your hand.
    🔀
    SWAP Tactical • Exchange
    Trades 1 random card from your hand with 1 random card from your opponent's hand, then ends your turn without drawing. Great for disrupting the opponent's strategy.
    🎴
    DRAW CARD Conclude Turn • Ends Turn
    Draws the top card from the deck into your hand and concludes your turn. If the card drawn is safe, your hand grows (+1 card). If it is the lethal HITMAN and you hold no SHIELD, you lose!

    💡 Tactical Pro-Tips for Victory

    1. Always play Utility cards first: Never click DRAW CARD while you still have SPY, STEAL, or SWAP in hand. Play them first for free value, then end your turn!
    2. Guard your SHIELD: Your SHIELD is your only passive defense against instant death. Avoid burning it as a manual skip unless your loss is otherwise guaranteed.
    3. Hitman Risk Percentage: Monitor the top HUD danger meter. At 25+ cards, risk is low (~4%). Below 10 cards, risk jumps above 10–20%. Save your ATTACK and SKIP cards for the late game!
    4. TRAP + ATTACK Combo: Playing TRAP followed by ATTACK forces your opponent into extreme danger with consecutive penalty draws.

    📖 How to Win & Master StratDeck

    💣 The Golden Rule: Avoid the Lethal HITMAN!
    StratDeck is a survival card game (Exploding Kittens / Russian Roulette mechanic). Drawing cards from the deck is a danger, not a prize! The draw deck contains a lethal HITMAN card. If any player draws the HITMAN without holding a SHIELD, they are instantly eliminated!
    ⚔️ How ATTACK Works: Forced Double Draw Risk
    Playing ATTACK immediately ends your turn without drawing and forces your opponent to take 2 consecutive turns (forcing them to draw twice from the minefield deck). If the opponent survives both draws without hitting the HITMAN, those safe cards are added to their hand (+2 cards). This is why their hand size increases — they survived 2 life-or-death draws! To steal cards directly from their hand, use STEAL.
    🎯 Secret #1: Save Your Defenses (SHIELD)
    Never waste SHIELD early! Hold SHIELD for when the RL Agent plays ATTACK or TRAP. If you have no SHIELD when ATTACK is played, you will be forced to draw multiple cards!
    ⚔️ Secret #2: Counter-Attack Hard
    When the RL Agent is low on hand cards (e.g. 1-2 cards), play ATTACK or TRAP! This forces the Agent to draw cards and ruins their tactical tempo.
    👁️ Secret #3: Use SPY for Free Info
    SPY does NOT end your turn! Play SPY first to peek at top deck cards before deciding whether to play ATTACK or DRAW.
    💰 Secret #4: Steal & Swap Disruptions
    STEAL takes a card directly from the Agent's hand. SWAP exchanges a random card. Use these when the AI Agent accumulates powerful cards!
